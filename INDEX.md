## 📋 Feature Overview

### Lead Management
- Create and manage leads
- Track pipeline status
- Automatic quality scoring
- Revenue tracking

### Scoring System
- Job type: 5-20 points
- Budget: 5-30 points
- Timeline: 3-25 points
- Property owner: 3-12 points
- Contact info: 0-15 points
- Scope detail: 0-5 points
- **Maximum: 100 points**

### Dashboard Tabs
1. **Board**: View and manage leads in pipeline
2. **New Lead**: Add new leads to system
3. **Numbers**: Revenue analytics and metrics

### Data Export
- Export to CSV format
- Share with team
- Analyze in Excel/Sheets
