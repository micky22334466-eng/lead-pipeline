✨ KEY FEATURES
================================================================================

📊 DASHBOARD (punchlist-lead-desk.html)
  • Lead Intake Form
  • Pipeline Board (New → Contacted → Quoted → Won → Dead)
  • Lead Scoring (0-100 scale)
  • Revenue Tracking
  • Source Analytics
  • CSV Export

🎯 LEAD SCORING
  • Job Type: 5-20 points (Remodel = highest)
  • Budget: 5-30 points (Higher = more points)
  • Timeline: 3-25 points (ASAP = highest)
  • Property Owner: 3-12 points (Owner > Manager > Renter)
  • Contact Info: 0-15 points (Phone + Email)
  • Scope Detail: 0-5 points (Detailed descriptions)
  • Maximum Score: 100 points

🔴 COLOR CODING
  • RED (70+): Hot lead - pursue immediately
  • GOLD (45-69): Warm lead - follow up soon
  • BLUE (0-44): Cold lead - nurture for later
